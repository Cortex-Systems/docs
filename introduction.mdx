The Cortex PQM is a DIN-rail mounted power quality monitor designed for installation inside electrical panels and distribution boards. It continuously measures voltage, current, power factor, energy consumption, and harmonic distortion up to the 63rd, giving engineers and facility managers the real-time and historical data they need to understand energy use, diagnose power quality issues, and meet reporting requirements. The device streams measurement data over a standard ethernet connection, making it straightforward to integrate into existing building management systems, data historians, or custom applications.

## What the Cortex PQM Measures

The Cortex PQM captures a comprehensive set of electrical measurements on every connected channel:

- **Voltage (V)** — RMS line voltage per phase, updated in real time
- **Current (A)** — RMS current per channel via CT clamp inputs
- **Active power (W) and reactive power (VAR)** — real and reactive load components
- **Apparent power (VA)** — total volt-ampere demand per channel
- **Power factor (PF)** — displacement and true power factor
- **Energy (kWh / kVARh)** — accumulated energy totals for billing and reporting
- **Harmonics (THD%)** — total harmonic distortion for voltage and current, with per-order harmonic magnitude up to the supported harmonic count
- **Frequency (Hz)** — mains frequency tracking

## Key Capabilities

**FLEX Port Expansion** The FLEX port slots on the device accept expansion CT modules, allowing you to increase the number of monitored current channels.

**AUX Port Modules** The AUX port supports accessory modules including digital I/O modules for pulse counting and relay outputs, and thermal probe modules for temperature monitoring.

**Modbus TCP Interface** For integration with PLCs, building management systems, and energy management systems that use Modbus.

**REST API** The Cortex PQM provides a documented REST API. You can query live measurements and manage configuration. The API is suitable for direct integration with custom dashboards, SCADA systems, cloud platforms, and more.

## How It Connects

The Cortex PQM mounts on a standard 35 mm DIN rail inside your electrical panel. Voltage inputs connect directly to the circuit conductors or bus bars via screw terminals on the device. Current is measured non-invasively using CT (current transformer) clamps that clip around the conductors  no circuit interruption is required to install them. A 12–24 VDC supply powers the device independently of the monitored circuits, ensuring continued monitoring and accurate phase reference even under variable load conditions. An RJ45 Ethernet port provides the network connection for data access and configuration.

## Typical Use Cases

- **Facility energy monitoring** — establish a baseline and track energy consumption across floors, departments, or cost centers
- **Sub-metering** — allocate energy costs to individual tenants, process lines, or equipment groups
- **BMS and SCADA integration** — feed real-time power data into building management or supervisory control systems via Modbus TCP or the REST API
- **Power quality analysis** — identify harmonic pollution, power factor problems, voltage sags, or phase imbalance that affect equipment performance and energy costs
- **Demand management** — monitor peak demand and trigger load-shedding signals via AUX port digital outputs
