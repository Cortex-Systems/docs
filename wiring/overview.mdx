The Cortex PQM CSM device requires four categories of wiring connections to operate correctly. Before you begin, read through this overview to understand what each connection type does and which pages cover the detailed instructions. Completing all four connection types in the correct order — starting with DC power last — reduces the risk of accidental energization during installation.

## Connection Summary

| Connection | Type | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| AC Voltage Inputs | Terminal block | Line voltage measurement |
| DC Power Supply | Terminal block | Device power (12–24VDC) |
| FLEX Port | Ribbon/direct | CT clamp inputs for current measurement |
| Ethernet | RJ45 | Network, REST API, configuration |

## General Wiring Safety Rules

Follow these rules for every connection type throughout the installation:

- **De-energize circuits** before making any connections to live conductors. Verify with a meter that the circuit is de-energized before touching terminals.
- **Use appropriate wire gauge** for each connection type. The minimum and maximum wire gauges for each terminal block are specified on the relevant sub-page.
- **Comply with local electrical codes.** All wiring must meet the requirements of your local jurisdiction, including conduit, labeling, and breaker sizing rules.
- **Use ferruled or tinned wire ends** on all terminal block connections to prevent stray strands from causing short circuits or intermittent contact.

<Warning>
  AC voltage wiring must be performed by a licensed electrician. Incorrect AC wiring can result in electric shock, equipment damage, or fire.
</Warning>
