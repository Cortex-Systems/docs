This page covers the DC power connection to the CSM monitor and the wiring of dry contact inputs on the digital input AUX modules.

---

## DC Power Wiring

- A DC power source is used to energize the monitor.
- The **ground (earth) connection for both AC and DC** is connected through the DC connector.
- A blinking green status LED indicates the status is good after power is applied.
- The monitor powers connected Flex Port and AUX modules through their Ethernet cables. AUX modules share a 500 mA budget.

<Note>
  Refer to the product label or datasheet for the DC supply voltage and current rating. See [DC Wiring Instructions](/csm/dc-wiring) for step-by-step instructions.
</Note>

---

## Digital Input Wiring (CS18-00-INC and CS18-02-SR1)

The CS18-00-INC Digital Input Module and the CS18-02-SR1 Digital I/O + Serial Module each provide 18 inputs for **voltage-free (dry) contacts**, such as breaker auxiliary contacts, door switches, and equipment status contacts.

### Specifications

| Parameter | Specification |
| --- | --- |
| Input type | Voltage-free (dry) contact |
| Closed threshold | Contact resistance below 1 kΩ |
| Terminals | Removable 3.5 mm terminal blocks, one common pin per input |
| External voltage | Not required |

### Wiring Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Connect the module to the AUX port">
    Connect the module's **IN** port to the monitor's AUX port (or to the previous module's **OUT** port) with a standard Ethernet cable.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Wire the contacts">
    Wire each dry contact across its input and common pins on the removable 3.5 mm terminal block. When running a shared common wire between multiple contacts, always land the common conductor on the common pin of each terminal.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Route field wiring">
    Route contact wiring away from high-voltage conductors where possible.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify operation">
    Check input status in the web console under **Data > Contacts**; closed contacts are highlighted in green.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Dry contacts are not polarity sensitive. Do not apply external voltage to the inputs.
</Note>

## Digital Outputs (CS18-02-SR1)

The CS18-02-SR1 provides 2 digital outputs rated **100 mA @ 30 VDC** max. They can be switched by Modbus command, via the REST API, or manually from the web console.
