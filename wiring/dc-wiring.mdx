The CSM device uses two types of DC wiring: a DC power supply that powers the device itself, and digital input wiring for the optional CS18-00-INC module, which accepts pulse or dry-contact signals from external meters and sensors. Complete the DC power supply wiring first, but do not energize the supply until all other wiring — AC voltage inputs, CT clamps, and Ethernet — is also complete.

---

## DC Power Supply Wiring

The CSM device requires a regulated DC supply to operate. Use a dedicated, regulated power supply rated for the CSM's current draw plus any installed expansion modules.

### Specifications

| Parameter | Specification |
|---|---|
| Supply voltage | 12–24VDC regulated |
| Typical current draw (no modules) | 500mA |
| Maximum current draw (all modules) | 1A |
| Terminal block wire range | 12–24 AWG |
| Recommended supply fusing | 2A |

### Wiring Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Size your power supply">
    Select a regulated 12–24VDC power supply rated for at least 1A output to accommodate the CSM and any installed modules.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Prepare the wires">
    Strip each wire to 7mm of bare conductor. If using stranded wire, terminate the end with a ferrule or tin it to prevent stray strands.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fuse the positive supply line">
    Insert a 2A fuse or circuit breaker in series with the positive DC supply line before it reaches the CSM terminal block.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect the positive terminal">
    Insert the positive supply wire (through the fuse) into the **[+]** terminal on the CSM and tighten the captive screw until firm.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect the negative terminal">
    Insert the negative (return) supply wire into the **[−]** terminal on the CSM and tighten the captive screw until firm.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify connections">
    Double-check polarity before applying power. With a multimeter, confirm the voltage at the terminals is within the 12–24VDC range.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Wiring Diagram

```text
DC Supply (+) ──┤2A fuse├── CSM [+] terminal
DC Supply (−) ─────────── CSM [−] terminal
```

<Warning>
  Reverse polarity on the DC power input may permanently damage the device and voids the warranty. Always verify polarity with a multimeter before applying power.
</Warning>

## Digital Input Wiring (CS18-00-INC Module)

The CS18-00-INC module adds digital input channels to the CSM, accepting dry contact closures or open-collector pulse signals from utility sub-meters, flow meters, gas meters, or any device with a pulse output.

### Specifications

| Parameter | Specification |
|---|---|
| Input type | Dry contact or open-collector |
| Recommended wire gauge | 22–26 AWG shielded |
| Maximum wire run | Refer to signal source spec; use shielded cable for runs over 10m |

### Wiring Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Identify the signal type">
    Determine whether your external device provides a dry contact (switch closure) or an open-collector pulse output. Both types wire the same way, but open-collector devices require the GND reference connection described below.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Run shielded cable">
    For long cable runs or installations near high-voltage conductors, use 22–26 AWG shielded twisted-pair cable. Route the cable away from AC power wiring where possible.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect the input wire">
    Connect one wire from the contact pair (or the pulse output **+** wire) to the desired **DI** input terminal on the CS18-00-INC module.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect the return/GND wire">
    Connect the other contact wire (or the pulse output **−** wire) to the **GND** terminal on the CS18-00-INC module.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Ground the shield (if applicable)">
    If using shielded cable, connect the shield drain wire to the **GND** terminal at the CSM end only. Leave the shield unconnected at the sensor end to avoid ground loops.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Wiring Diagram

```text
Meter Pulse (+) ─────────── DI1 terminal (CS18-00-INC)
Meter Pulse (−) ─────────── GND terminal (CS18-00-INC)
```

<Note>
  For dry contact inputs, either wire of the contact pair can connect to the DI terminal — polarity does not matter for dry contacts. Polarity does matter for open-collector pulse outputs; always connect the labeled **+** to the DI terminal and **−** to GND.
</Note>
