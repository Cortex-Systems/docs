## Checking Your Current Firmware Version

Before deciding whether an update is needed, confirm the version currently running on your device

**Via the web interface:**

1. Navigate to your device's IP address in a browser.
2. Go to **Support → Information**
3. The firmware version is displayed under **Firmware Version**

## Update Methods

Two methods are available for updating firmware on the Cortex PQM:

<table>
  <colgroup>
    <col width="144" />
    <col width="268" />
    <col width="100" />
    <col width="271" />

  <thead>
    <tr>
      <th>Method</th>
      <th>Requirements</th>
      <th>Downtime</th>
      <th>Best For</th>
    </tr>
  </thead>
  <tbody>
    <tr>
      <td>USB Flash Drive</td>
      <td>USB flash drive, FAT32 formatted</td>
      <td>~3 min</td>
      <td>No network access, field updates</td>
    </tr>
    <tr>
      <td>Network</td>
      <td>Ethernet connected and firmware file</td>
      <td>~3 min</td>
      <td>Remote updates, multiple devices</td>
    </tr>
  </tbody>
</table>

<Warning>
  Do not power off the device during a firmware update. Interrupting the update process may corrupt the firmware and require a recovery procedure.
</Warning>
