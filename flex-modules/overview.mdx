The FLEX port system lets you expand the current measurement capacity of your Cortex PQM CSM device by snapping in dedicated CT (current transformer) modules. Each module plugs directly into a FLEX port slot on the front of the CSM-1-00 or CSM-2-00, and the device recognises the new channels automatically — no firmware update or manual channel mapping is required at the hardware level.

## Available FLEX Modules

Three FLEX module types are available, each suited to a different installation scenario:

- **CT Card Module (FLEX)** — A general-purpose expansion card that adds 6 or 12 individual CT input channels. Use this card when you need to monitor a small to medium number of discrete circuits using split-core or solid-core clamp-on CTs.
- **Solid Core CT Strips** — Pre-wired arrays of solid-core current transformers sized to fit inside 42–48 or 72–84 pole electrical panels. Pair these strips with a CT Card Module or the Multi-Circuit Card.
- **Multi-Circuit Card (CS12-42-5-SA1)** — A single FLEX module that accepts a CT strip ribbon cable and monitors up to 42 circuits simultaneously. Designed for large panels where installing individual clamp-on CTs is impractical.

## How the FLEX Port Works

FLEX modules connect directly to the keyed edge-connector slots on the CSM device. When you insert a module and power on the device, the CSM enumerates the attached cards and exposes their channels through the REST API and configuration interface without additional setup.

The number of FLEX slots available depends on your device model:

| Device Model | FLEX Slots |
|---|---|
| CSM-1-00 | 1 |
| CSM-2-00 | 2 |

<Warning>
Only use Cortex Systems approved FLEX modules. Third-party modules are not electrically or mechanically compatible and may damage the device or produce inaccurate measurements. Using unapproved modules voids the product warranty.
</Warning>
