The CSM-1-00 and CSM-2-00 mount on a standard 35 mm DIN rail. Flex Port and AUX modules are separate DIN-rail devices that connect to the monitor with standard Ethernet cables, so plan rail space and cable routes for them as well.

## Requirements

- Standard 35 mm DIN rail, securely fastened inside the enclosure
- Access to the DC connector, AC reference voltage inputs, Flex Ports, AUX port, Ethernet ports, USB port, and reset button after mounting

## Mounting Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Plan the layout">
    Plan space on the rail for the monitor and any CT modules and AUX modules. Modules connect to the monitor using standard Ethernet cables up to 100 ft (33 m), so they can be placed close to the circuits or contacts they monitor.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Hook the monitor onto the rail">
    Hook the top of the monitor's DIN clip over the top edge of the rail.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Snap the monitor into place">
    Press the bottom of the monitor toward the rail until the clip snaps over the bottom edge of the rail.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify the monitor is secure">
    Confirm the monitor does not move or pull away from the rail.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm access to ports">
    Confirm that the Flex Ports, AUX port, Ethernet ports, USB port, and reset button remain accessible.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  AC wiring has lethal voltages. Installation must be completed by a qualified person.
</Warning>
