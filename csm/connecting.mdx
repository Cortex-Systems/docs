---

After mounting the monitor on the DIN rail, make the power, voltage, module, and network connections.

<Warning>
  AC wiring has lethal voltages. Installation must be completed by a qualified person. De-energize circuits and verify they are de-energized before making voltage connections.
</Warning>

## Connection Sequence

<Steps>
  <Step title="Connect the DC power source">
    Connect the DC power source to the DC connector. The ground (earth) connection for both AC and DC is made through the DC connector. See [DC Wiring](/csm/dc-wiring).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect the AC reference voltage">
    Connect the AC reference voltage to Volt Input 1 (and Volt Input 2 on the CSM-2-00). The inputs are for sensing only and draw no power. Fuse according to code, 0.25 A up to the maximum conductor rating. See [AC Wiring](/wiring/ac-wiring).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect CT devices to the Flex Ports">
    Connect CT modules, CT strips, or Multi-Cards to Flex Ports 1–4 using standard Ethernet cables. Connect the Flex Port to the **IN** port of the first device, and **OUT** to **IN** for each following device, up to 48 current channels per port. Start with Flex Port 1. Connect 0.333 V CTs to the 3.5 mm headers on the CT modules. See [Flex Port Wiring](/wiring/flex-port).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect AUX modules">
    Connect Digital Input, Digital I/O + Serial, and Thermal modules to the **AUX** port using standard Ethernet cables, **IN** of the first module to the AUX port and **OUT** to **IN** for each following module. See [AUX Port Modules](/aux-modules/overview).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect Ethernet">
    Connect Ethernet Port 1 or 2 to your PC or network. Both ports share a common IP address (default `192.168.7.3`). Do not connect the network to a Flex Port or the AUX port. See [Ethernet Wiring](/wiring/ethernet).
  </Step>
</Steps>

## After Connecting

Apply power and confirm:

- The status LED on the monitor blinks green (status good).
- The green LED on each Flex Port and AUX module blinks, indicating communication.
- You can open the web console at the monitor's IP address.

Then configure the panelboard in the web console under **Configuration > Meter Config > Step 1**.
