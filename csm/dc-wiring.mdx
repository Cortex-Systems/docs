The CSM-1-00 and CSM-2-00 are energized by a DC power source connected to the DC connector. The monitor does not draw power from the AC reference voltage inputs, which are for sensing only.

## Key Points

- A DC power source is used to energize the monitor.
- The **ground (earth) connection for both AC and DC** is connected through the DC connector.
- Flex Port modules and AUX modules are powered by the monitor through their Ethernet cables; no separate supply is needed for the modules.
- AUX modules have a combined current budget of **500 mA** (Digital Input module 125 mA, Digital I/O + Serial module 82 mA, Thermal module 50 mA). See [AUX Port Modules](/aux-modules/overview).

<Note>
  Refer to the product label or datasheet for the DC supply voltage and current rating of your monitor.
</Note>

## Wiring Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Switch off the DC supply">
    Confirm the DC power source is off before wiring.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect the DC supply and ground">
    Connect the DC supply conductors and the ground (earth) conductor to the DC connector, observing the polarity markings on the monitor.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify the connections">
    Check polarity and confirm each conductor is secure in the connector.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Apply power">
    Switch on the DC supply. A blinking green status LED indicates the status is good.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## After Powering On

Once the status LED is blinking green, connect to the web console at the monitor's IP address (default `http://192.168.7.3`). See the Quick Start Guide and the [Quick Reference](/quick-reference#status-led) for status LED indications.
