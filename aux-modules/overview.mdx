AUX modules extend the monitoring capabilities of your Cortex monitor with up to eight daisy-chained cards, providing digital input, digital output, RS-485, and temperature monitoring.

## Available AUX Modules

| Module | Part Number | Function | Current draw |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [Digital Input Module](/aux-modules/digital-input) | CS18-00-INC | 18 dry contact inputs | 125 mA |
| [Digital I/O Module + Serial](/aux-modules/digital-io-serial) | CS18-02-SR1 | 18 dry contact inputs, 2 digital outputs, RS-485 Modbus RTU | 82 mA |
| [Thermal Module](/aux-modules/thermal-module) | CS18-01-TMP | RTD temperature sensors | 50 mA |

## Connection Guide

AUX modules connect to the monitor's **AUX port** using a standard Ethernet cable. Do **not** connect them to the Flex Ports.

1. Always connect the **IN** port of the first module to the monitor's AUX port.
2. Connect the module's **OUT** port to the **IN** port of the next AUX port device.
3. When a module is connected to the monitor, the green LED on the card blinks to show that it is communicating.

All field wiring terminates on removable 3.5 mm terminal blocks. The sequence of the modules in the chain determines their register assignments.

## Daisy Chaining

AUX modules connect to the CSM-1-00 or CSM-2-00 using standard Ethernet cables up to 100 ft (33 m). Up to 8 AUX modules may be daisy-chained from the AUX port, provided the combined current draw of all connected modules does not exceed **500 mA**.

### Maximum Quantity by Module Type

When a chain consists of a single module type, the 500 mA budget limits the number of modules as follows:

| Module | Maximum in a single-type chain |
| --- | --- |
| Digital Input Module (CS18-00-INC) | 4 |
| Digital I/O Module + Serial (CS18-02-SR1) | 6 |
| Thermal Module (CS18-01-TMP) | 8 |

For chains that combine module types, total the current draw of each module and confirm the sum remains at or below 500 mA.

## Firmware Requirements

- The CSM-1 or CSM-2 must have firmware version 1.9 / 2.9 or higher to communicate with the CS18-02-SR1.
- The maximum number of daisy-chained modules also depends on firmware:
  - Firmware earlier than 2.9.1: 4 modules maximum
  - Firmware 2.9.1 or later: 8 modules maximum
