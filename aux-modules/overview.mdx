## Connection Guide

<Steps>
  <Step title="Connect to the **AUX** port of the CSM Monitor">
    Connect the **IN** port of the first module to the monitor's **AUX port** using a standard ethernet cable. Do **not** connect it to the Flex Ports.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Daisy-chain additional devices">
    The first modules **OUT** port can then be connected to the **IN** port of the next module.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify communication">
    When the module is connected to the monitor, the green LED on the card blinks to show that it is communicating.
  </Step>
</Steps>

![Image](/images/image-44.png "Image")

<Note>
  All field wiring terminates on removable 3.5 mm terminal blocks. The sequence of the modules in the chain determines their register assignments.
</Note>

## **Daisy Chaining Limitations**

Connects to the Cortex CSM-1-00 or CSM-2-00 using CAT6A ethernet cables up to 100’ (33m).<br />Up to 8x AUX modules may be daisy-chained from the AUX port, provided the combined current draw of all connected modules does not exceed a total of 500 mA.

#### **Module Current Draw**

- Digital Input Module: CS18-00-INC
  - 125 mA
- Digital I/O Module + Serial: CS18-02-SR1
  - 82 mA
- Thermal Module: CS18-01-TMP
  - 50 mA

#### **Maximum Quantity by Module Type**

When a chain consists of a single module type, the 500 mA budget limits the number of<br />modules as follows:

- Digital Input Module: 4 maximum
- Digital I/O Module + Serial: 6 maximum
- Thermal Module: 8 maximum

For chains that combine module types, total the current draw of each module and confirm the<br />sum remains at or below 500 mA.

![](https://manula.r.sizr.io/large/user/23498/img/aux-power-budget.png)

## Firmware Requirements

- The CSM-1 or CSM-2 must have firmware version 1.9 / 2.9 or higher to communicate with the CS18-02-SR1.
- The maximum number of daisy-chained modules also depends on firmware:
  - Firmware earlier than 2.9.1: 4 modules maximum
  - Firmware 2.9.1 or later: 8 modules maximum
