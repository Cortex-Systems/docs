AUX modules are separate devices that connect to the monitor's AUX port with standard Ethernet cables. Because they do not plug directly into the monitor, they can be placed close to the contacts or sensors they monitor.

<Note>
  Refer to the individual product datasheets for mechanical drawings and dimensions. Contact Cortex Systems if you require CAD files for enclosure design.
</Note>

## Module Summary

| Module | Part Number | Current draw | Field wiring |

| Thermal Module | CS18-01-TMP | 50 mA | Removable 3.5 mm terminal blocks |

## Mounting and Cabling Considerations

- **Mounting** — AUX modules mount on standard 35 mm DIN rail.
- **Cable length** — Modules connect using standard Ethernet cables up to 100 ft (33 m).
- **Daisy chaining** — Up to 8 modules may be chained (firmware 2.9.1 or later), within a 500 mA combined current budget.
- **Wiring space** — Allow clearance for terminal block wiring and probe cables.
