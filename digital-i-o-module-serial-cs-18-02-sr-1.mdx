### **Connecting the DI/DO + Serial Module to the Meter**

The DI/DO module connects to the meter’s **AUX port** using a standard Ethernet cable. Do **not** connect it to the Flex ports.

Always connect the **IN** port of the first module to the meter’s AUX port. The module’s **OUT** port can then be connected to the **IN** port of the next AUX port device (such as a DI card, Thermal Card, or Multi-Card), as shown in the diagram below.

When the module is connected to the meter, the green LED on the card blinks to show that it is communicating.

![Image](/images/image-5.png "Image")

All field wiring terminates on removable 3.5 mm terminal blocks. Each card provides:

- 18 digital inputs (dry contact)
- 2 digital outputs (100 mA @ 30 VDC max.)
- 1 RS-485 serial port (2-wire Modbus RTU)

![Image](/images/image-6.png "Image")

### **Digital Inputs**

The digital inputs are designed for voltage-free (dry) contacts. An input reads as closed when the resistance across its contacts drops below 1 kΩ.

The first pin on each terminal block is common. When one common wire serves several contacts, always land it on the first pin.

![Image](/images/image-7.png "Image")

You can also check input status in the meter’s web console under **Data > Contacts**. Closed contacts are highlighted in green.

![Image](/images/image-8.png "Image")

### **Digital Outputs**

The digital outputs are rated for 100 mA @ 30 VDC. They can be switched by Modbus command or manually from the HTML console. A future firmware release will add the option to switch outputs on alarm conditions.

### **Digital Input Registers**

Digital input registers are grouped in sequential blocks, one per card. Cards are numbered 1 through 8 in the order they are connected, starting with the card plugged directly into the AUX port (Card 1). A card’s position in the chain decides which register block it uses.

**Individual input registers:** Each of the 18 inputs has its own status register. For Card 1, these start at register 2046 (Input 1) and run through 2063 (Input 18). A value of 0 means the contact is open, and 1 means it is closed.

![Image](/images/image-10.png "Image")

**Block read registers:** The status of all 18 inputs on a card can also be read from a two-register block. For Card 1, this block is registers 2001–2002:

- Register 2001, bits 0–15: Inputs 1–16
- Register 2002, bit 0: Input 17
- Register 2002, bit 1: Input 18

A bit value of 1 means the contact is closed, and 0 means it is open. Each following card uses the next two registers (Card 2: 2003–2004, Card 3: 2005–2006, and so on through Card 8: 2015–2016).

### **Digital Output Registers**

Digital output registers are also grouped by card number. Card numbers are assigned by position in the chain, and Card 1 is the card closest to the AUX port. **Every** device connected to the AUX port counts as a card when numbers are assigned, including Thermal Cards and Multi-Cards.
