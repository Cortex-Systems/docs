The Cortex Power Quality Monitor (PQM) is a DIN-rail mounted energy monitoring device designed for electrical panels. The CSM-1-00 and CSM-2-00 models measure voltage, current, and power quality parameters across your facility, and expose data through a REST API and Modbus interface for integration with SCADA systems, building management software, and custom dashboards.

## What's in This Manual

This manual covers everything you need to install, configure, and integrate the Cortex PQM family of power quality monitors.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Install the Hardware">
    Mount the CSM device on your DIN rail and connect it to your electrical panel. See [CSM Device Setup](/csm/overview) and the [Wiring Guide](/wiring/overview).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure the Device">
    Upload or edit configuration files to set up circuit names, CT ratios, network settings, and more. See [Configuration](/configuration/overview).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect FLEX and AUX Modules">
    Expand monitoring capability by adding CT card modules, solid core CT strips, digital I/O, or thermal probes. See [FLEX Port Modules](/flex-modules/overview) and [AUX Port Modules](/aux-modules/overview).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Integrate with Your Systems">
    Pull live power data from the device's [REST API](/api/overview) or read Modbus registers directly from your [SCADA or BMS](/modbus/register-map).
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Device Overview

The Cortex PQM family includes two main device variants:

| Model | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| **CSM-1-00** | Single-phase power quality monitor with FLEX and AUX expansion ports |
| **CSM-2-00** | Three-phase power quality monitor with FLEX and AUX expansion ports |

Both models support the same expansion modules, configuration system, REST API, and Modbus interface.

<Note>
  Always follow your local electrical codes and safety regulations when installing the Cortex PQM. Work performed inside electrical panels must be carried out by a qualified electrician.
</Note>
