The Cortex Power Quality Monitor (PQM) is a DIN-rail mounted monitor for electrical panels, PDUs, and distribution equipment. The CSM-1-00 and CSM-2-00 measure voltage, current, power, energy, and power quality on up to 192 circuits, and expose data through a web console, a REST API, and Modbus TCP for integration with SCADA systems, building management software, and custom dashboards.

## What's in This Manual

<Steps>
  <Step title="Install the Hardware">
    Mount the monitor on a DIN rail and connect DC power, AC reference voltage, and Ethernet. See [CSM Device Setup](/csm/overview) and the [Wiring Guide](/wiring/overview).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect Flex Port and AUX Modules">
    Connect CT modules and CT strips to the four Flex Ports (up to 48 current channels each), and digital I/O and thermal modules to the AUX port. See [FLEX Port Modules](/flex-modules/overview) and [AUX Port Modules](/aux-modules/overview).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure the Device">
    Configure network settings, panelboard layout, CT sizes, and circuits in the web console or with configuration files. See [Configuration](/configuration/overview).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Integrate with Your Systems">
    Read data from the [REST API](/api/overview) or poll [Modbus registers](/modbus/register-map) from your SCADA or BMS.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Device Overview

| Model | Description |
| --- | --- |
| **CSM-1-00** | Power quality monitor with one voltage input, four Flex Ports, and an AUX port |
| **CSM-2-00** | Power quality monitor with two voltage inputs, four Flex Ports, and an AUX port |

Both models support the same expansion modules, configuration system, REST API, and Modbus interface.

<Note>
  AC wiring has lethal voltages. Installation must be completed by a qualified person in accordance with local electrical codes.
</Note>
